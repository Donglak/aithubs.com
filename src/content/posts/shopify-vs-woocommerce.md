**"Shopify is easier. WooCommerce is cheaper."**

That's the surface-level take. It's also **dangerously incomplete**.

I've migrated 50+ stores between platforms. The businesses that choose wrong don't just waste money—they lose momentum, customers, and sometimes the business itself.

The real difference isn't features. It's **total cost of ownership at YOUR scale**, **technical debt trajectory**, and **exit options**.

> 🎯 **By the end:** You'll have a scored decision matrix for YOUR situation, the hidden costs nobody mentions, and a migration roadmap if you're switching.

## The 60-Second Verdict

| Choose **Shopify** If... | Choose **WooCommerce** If... |
|--------------------------|------------------------------|
| You want to SELL, not maintain | You want CONTROL, not convenience |
| Revenue < $1M/year (usually) | Revenue > $1M/year (usually) |
| No developer on team | Developer on team (or budget for one) |
| Standard products, simple variants | Complex products, subscriptions, B2B |
| Speed to market > customization | Customization > speed to market |
| You'll stay 3-5 years max | You're building a 10-year asset |

**Still unsure?** Keep reading. The nuance is worth $50K+.

## Migration Scenarios: The Real Pain Points

### Scenario 1: Starting Fresh (No Data)

**Shopify:** 1-2 days to launch. Pick theme, add products, connect Stripe, go.
**WooCommerce:** 3-7 days. Hosting, WP install, theme, plugins, configure, test, go.

**Winner:** Shopify for speed. WooCommerce if you need custom from day 1.

---

### Scenario 2: Migrating FROM Shopify TO WooCommerce

**Data Export:** Products, customers, orders → CSV (Shopify admin)
**Migration Tools:** Cart2Cart ($69-500), LitExtension ($100-1,000), or custom script
**Pain Points:**
- Order history URLs change (SEO risk)
- Customer passwords don't migrate (reset required)
- Subscription contracts need manual recreation
- App data (reviews, wishlists, loyalty) = manual or lost

**Timeline:** 2-4 weeks for clean migration
**Cost:** $2,000-15,000 depending on complexity

---

### Scenario 3: Migrating FROM WooCommerce TO Shopify

**Data Export:** WP All Export, custom queries
**Migration Tools:** Same tools, reverse direction
**Pain Points:**
- Custom fields → Metafields (mapping hell)
- Complex product structures → Shopify limits
- Custom checkout flows → Lost (unless Plus)
- SEO URLs → Redirect mapping critical
- Plugin functionality → App subscriptions ($$$)

**Timeline:** 3-6 weeks
**Cost:** $5,000-30,000 (higher due to rebuild needs)

> ⚠️ **Migration Reality:** Budget 3x what agencies quote. Plan 2x timeline. Run parallel 30 days.

## Real Store Case Studies

### **Store A: DTC Apparel — $2M/yr → Shopify Plus**
- **Why:** 3-person team, no dev, multi-channel (POS, Instagram, TikTok Shop)
- **Cost:** $2,500/mo + apps
- **Pain:** Limited B2B, variant limits
- **Verdict:** Right call. Team focuses on marketing, not tech.

### **Store B: Industrial Supplies — $5M/yr → WooCommerce**
- **Why:** 50K SKUs, complex pricing tiers, ERP integration, 2 devs
- **Cost:** $3,000/mo hosting + $15K/yr dev
- **Pain:** Plugin management, security
- **Verdict:** Right call. Custom pricing engine impossible on Shopify.

### **Store C: Supplements — $500K/yr → Migrated Shopify → WooCommerce**
- **Trigger:** Subscription revenue >60%, needed custom bundles
- **Migration:** 6 weeks, $18K
- **Savings:** $1,200/mo apps → $200/yr plugins
- **ROI:** Break-even month 8

### **Store D: Fashion Marketplace — $10M/yr → Headless (Shopify + Next.js)**
- **Why:** Performance, SEO, custom UX, global expansion
- **Architecture:** Shopify backend (checkout, orders) + Next.js frontend
- **Cost:** $2,300/mo Shopify + $50K/yr dev team
- **Verdict:** Best of both. But only at scale.

## 🎯 Your 10-Minute Decision Checklist

**Right now, answer YES/NO:**

1. [ ] Do you have a developer (or $2K+/mo budget)?
2. [ ] Is revenue > $500K/year OR growing >100%/year?
3. [ ] Do you need complex products (B2B, subscriptions, custom)?
4. [ ] Is data ownership / exit strategy important?
5. [ ] Will you need heavy customization in 2+ years?
6. [ ] Can you handle 2-4 weeks of setup time?
7. [ ] Do you want full control over performance/SEO?

**5+ YES → WooCommerce**
**3-4 YES → Either (lean WooCommerce if dev available)**
**0-2 YES → Shopify**
